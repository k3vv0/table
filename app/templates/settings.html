    Send to Plate (iOS Shortcut)

    Share a link or some text from any app straight into your Plate's inbox. Each phone gets its own token; revoke one and only that phone stops working.

    Copy this now; it won't be shown again.

      How to build the Shortcut
      1. In Shortcuts, make a new shortcut. In its details, turn on Show in Share Sheet and accept URLs and Text.
      2. Add Get Contents of URL: URL https://dash.sangmuah.net/api/capture, Method POST, Request Body JSON.
      3. Headers: CF-Access-Client-Id and CF-Access-Client-Secret from the table-capture service token, and X-Capture-Token with the token above.
      4. JSON fields: text = Shortcut Input, and optionally to = table for a household version.
      5. Add Show Notification "Sent to Plate" so you know it landed.
